
- [note]: information {{assistant}} brings (a premise or date changed, which to-do it concerns). The matching to-do is
  the project's own wording; match by meaning. If it affects that to-do, rewrite it according to the fact; otherwise
  record it in the related section. A note does not mean the work is done: do not delete a to-do because of a note.
- [dismissed]: {{owner}} decided this question needs no answer. Remove the to-do waiting on the answer; record why if
  that is useful.
- [same_as]: {{assistant}} merged this item with the same one in another project; for reference only, the to-dos stay
  as they are.
- "The assistant's reasoning" says who decided and on what grounds: if {{owner}} decided, write it that way; if
  {{assistant}} concluded it from evidence, mark it in STATE.md as an inference, and do not write it as something
  {{owner}} said.
